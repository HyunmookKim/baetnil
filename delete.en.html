1. Delete it inside the app

  1. Open the app and tap ≡ at the top right
  2. Tap Account
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom
  4. Check what will be removed, then type the confirmation word exactly as shown
Before deleting, Save to a file first lets you keep all your records as one file.

2. No app? Ask by email

Write to the address below with the email address you signed up with and the words "account deletion request". We delete it once we have confirmed it is you.
Request by email

How long it takes — we delete it within 30 days of your request and tell you when it is done.

3. What is deleted

Deleted Your sign-in account (email, name, account identifier)
Every boat you own and all of its records — stores, maintenance, repairs, fuel, logbook, documents, deck plans, board
Community posts and comments you wrote, moorings, marketplace listings
Photos you uploaded
The device token kept so notifications could reach you
The records stored on this device
Kept If you only joined someone else's boat, that boat's records are not deleted. Your name may remain on its crew list, so it is cleaner to ask that boat's owner to remove you first.
Records the law requires us to keep are held for that period only: support enquiries for 3 years, reports and enforcement records for 3 years (Privacy policy, section 4).

4. This cannot be undone

Once deleted, records cannot be brought back. If you may want them, use Settings › Save to a file first.
