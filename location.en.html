Article 1 (Purpose)
These Terms set out how Baetnil (the "App") handles location information.

Article 2 (What it is used for)
 · to show wind, waves, tides and marine warnings for where you are
 · to pick the tide station nearest the boat's home port and convert a measured depth
   to the lowest-tide datum
 · to show only the warnings for the waters the boat is in
 · to post berths you mark on the map to the community
 · to draw the routes you record in the logbook

Article 3 (Current position)
1. Your current position (GPS) is received only when all three of the following hold:
   (a) you are signed in, (b) you have agreed to the use of location information, and
   (c) you have permitted it on your device.
   The current position of someone who is not signed in is never received.
   (Home port, berths and weather spots can be marked on the map without signing in.)
2. What happens to the value depends on how it is used:
   a. to set a weather spot - it stays on your device and is not sent to the server;
   b. to record a departure, waypoint or arrival in the logbook - it is stored on the
      server as part of that voyage record.
3. You can use the whole App if you refuse. Simply choose a spot yourself.
4. You may withdraw your consent at any time under [Menu] - [Terms & Privacy] - [Location],
   or in your device settings.
5. Under Article 24(2) of the Location Information Act you may also pause collection at any time.
   Turning the same switch off stops collection from that moment; turning it back on resumes it.
   We do not prevent you from switching it off and on.

Article 4 (Coordinates stored on the server)
1. Home port and berth coordinates are values you mark on the map yourself.
2. Voyage coordinates are current position (GPS) values captured when you record a
   departure, waypoint or arrival. They are shown on screen, and you can correct them on
   the map or remove them with Delete.
3. Voyage coordinates can be seen only by that boat's crew. They are never published even
   if the boat is made public.
4. Berth coordinates are published to the community. You can check them on screen before
   posting, and you can delete them afterwards.

Article 5 (Retention)
1. Stored coordinates are kept until you delete them, or until you leave, and are then
   destroyed.
2. The confirmation data described in Article 6 is kept for six months from the time it is
   recorded, and is then erased automatically.

Article 6 (Confirmation data on collection, use and provision)
1. As required by Article 16(2) of the Location Information Act, the App automatically
   records the fact each time location information is collected, used or provided.
   Nothing about this is done by hand.
2. Only three things are recorded: when, what it was used for, and whether it was a
   collection, a use or a provision. Coordinates are not recorded in the confirmation data,
   because what the law requires is a record of when location was used, not the location.
3. This record is kept both on your device and on the server. Nobody but you can read the
   copy on the server - not even the operator.
4. Records older than six months are erased automatically.

Article 7 (Requesting to read or be told your confirmation data)
Under Article 24 of the Location Information Act you may ask to read, or to be told, your own
confirmation data. Contact the location information manager named in Article 11 below and we
will look it up and send it to you.
You can withdraw your consent yourself in the App under
[Menu] - [Terms & Privacy] - [Location].

Article 8 (Disclosure to third parties and notification)
1. We do not give your location information to anyone else. We comply only with lawful demands
   made under the law.
2. If we ever provide personal location information to a third party you have designated, we will
   tell you who received it, when, and why, each time and immediately, as required by Article 19(3)
   of the Location Information Act.

Article 9 (Your rights)
You may withdraw consent to the collection, use and provision of your location information
at any time, and you may ask to see or delete what is stored. Contact: help@baetnil.com

Article 10 (Children under 14, and children aged 8 or under)
1. This app does not accept members under 14, so we do not take their location information either.
2. This app does not separately handle the location information of the children aged 8 or under,
   adults under guardianship, or persons with severe mental disabilities referred to in Article 26 of
   the Location Information Act. There is no feature that locates such a person with a guardian's consent.
