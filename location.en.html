Article 1 (Purpose)
These Terms set out how Baetnil (the "App") handles location information.

Article 2 (What it is used for)
 · to show wind, waves, tides and marine warnings for where you are
 · to pick the tide station nearest the boat's home port and convert a measured depth
   to chart datum
 · to show only the warnings for the waters the boat is in
 · to post the marina and anchorage locations you mark on the map to the community
 · to draw the routes you record in the logbook

Article 3 (Current position)
1. Your current position (GPS) is received only when all three of the following are true:
   (a) you are signed in, (b) you have agreed to the use of location information, and
   (c) you have allowed it on your device.
   The current position of someone who is not signed in is never received.
   (You can mark a home port, a marina or anchorage, or a weather spot on the map without signing in.)
2. What happens to the value depends on how it is used:
   a. to set a weather spot — it stays on your device and is not sent to the server;
   b. to record a departure, log entry or arrival in the logbook — it is stored on the
      server as part of that voyage record.
3. You can still use the App if you decline. Simply choose a spot yourself.
4. You may withdraw your consent at any time under [Menu] - [Terms & Privacy] - [Location],
   or in your device settings.
5. Under Article 24(2) of the Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information
   (the "Location Information Act"), you may also pause collection at any time.
   Turning the same switch off stops collection from that moment; turning it back on resumes it.
   We do not prevent you from switching it off and on.

Article 4 (Coordinates stored on the server)
1. Home port and marina or anchorage coordinates are values you mark on the map yourself.
2. Voyage coordinates are current position (GPS) values captured when you record a
   departure, log entry or arrival. They are shown on screen, and you can correct them on
   the map or remove them with Delete.
3. Voyage coordinates can be seen only by that boat's crew. They are never published even
   if the boat is made public.
4. Marina and anchorage coordinates are published to the community. You can check them on
   screen before posting, and you can delete them afterward.

Article 5 (Purpose of retention, retention period, and the basis for it)
1. The purpose of holding personal location data is only what Article 2 sets out: to get
   the weather and tides for that location, to convert a measured depth to chart datum, to show
   only the warnings for those waters, and to draw the marina and anchorage locations and voyage
   tracks you record. There is no other purpose.
2. Stored coordinates are kept until you delete them or until you leave, and are then
   destroyed. They are destroyed when the purpose is met or when you withdraw consent.
3. The location-use records in Article 6 are kept because
   Article 16(2) of the Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information (Republic of Korea)
   requires them to be automatically recorded and preserved in the location information system.
   That is the basis for retaining them.
4. The retention period for location-use records is six months. They are erased automatically
   six months after they are recorded. No law requires them to be kept longer.

Article 6 (Records of collection, use and disclosure)
1. As required by Article 16(2) of the Location Information Act, the App automatically
   records the fact each time location information is collected, used or disclosed.
   None of this is done by hand.
2. Only three things are recorded: when, for what purpose, and whether it was collection,
   use or disclosure. Coordinates are not included in these records, because what the law
   requires is a record that location was used, not the location itself.
3. This record is kept both on your device and on the server. Nobody but you can read the
   copy on the server — not even the operator.
4. Records older than six months are erased automatically.

Article 7 (Requesting access to or notice of your records)
Under Article 24 of the Location Information Act, you may request to view, or to be notified of,
your own location-use records. Contact the location information manager named in Article 11
below, and the operator will look them up and send them to you.
You can withdraw your consent yourself in the App under
[Menu] - [Terms & Privacy] - [Location].

Article 8 (Disclosure to third parties and notification)
1. The operator does not give your location information to anyone else. It does so only when
   legally required.
2. If the operator ever provides personal location information to a third party you have
   designated, it will tell you who received it, when, and why, immediately and each time,
   as required by Article 19(3) of the Location Information Act.

Article 9 (Your rights)
You may withdraw consent to the collection, use and disclosure of your location information
at any time, and you may ask to see or delete what is stored. Contact: help@baetnil.com

Article 10 (Children under 14, and children aged 8 or under)
1. The App does not accept members under 14, so it does not collect their location information either.
2. The App does not separately handle the location information of children aged 8 or under,
   adults under guardianship, or persons with severe mental disabilities referred to in Article 26 of
   the Location Information Act. There is no feature that locates such a person with a guardian's consent.

Article 12 (Charges and conditions)
1. There is no charge for the collection, use or disclosure of location information.
   All of it is free.
2. You can use the App without sharing your location. Simply choose a weather spot yourself.
   Sharing your location is not a condition of use.
3. If charges are introduced in the future, they will be announced in the App beforehand, and
   your consent will be sought again. The operator will not start charging for anything you
   already use for free without notice.
